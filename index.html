Kids' Dentistry

Pediatric Health Services

At Family Dental Care, our children's dental care includes:

  • Routine dental examinations
  • Professional dental cleaning
  • Cavity detection and treatment
  • Fluoride-related preventive care when appropriate
  • Monitoring tooth and jaw development
  • Guidance for parents on brushing and oral hygiene
  • Evaluation of dental injuries and tooth-related pain

Dental Aesthetics

Scaling, Polishing & Teeth Whitening

We provide dental aesthetic treatments designed to help improve the appearance of your smile while keeping your oral health at the center of your treatment plan.Our dental aesthetics services includes:

  • Smile makeover consultations
  • Professional scaling
  • Dental polishing
  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Assessment of tooth discoloration
  • Personalized smile improvement plans

pre Assessment

Why Have an Assessment Before Cosmetic Treatment?

Before beginning aesthetic treatment at Family Dental Care, we check for issues such as cavities, gum disease, significant sensitivity, or other oral health problems.
Treating these conditions first can help ensure that cosmetic treatment is carried out appropriately.

Orthodontics

Our orthodontic services include assessment and treatment planning for concerns such as:

  • Crowded teeth
  • Crooked Teeth
  • Gap between teeth
  • Overbite
  • Underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Certain bite irregularities
  • Teeth that are difficult to clean because of their position

Treatments

Treatment Options

During your consultation, we assess your teeth and bite and discuss the treatment options that may be appropriate for your case.

Braces
Patients normally attend regular appointments so our dental team can monitor progress and make necessary adjustments.
Clear Aligners
Clear aligners are removable, customized trays that gradually move teeth according to a planned treatment sequence.
Patients need to follow the recommended wearing schedule carefully to achieve the planned tooth movements.

Prosthodontics

Zirconia Crowns, PFM Crowns, Bridges and Dentures

Our prosthodontic services include dental crowns and bridges, with crown options such as zirconia and porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) along with custom dentures, providing a practical solution for patients with multiple missing teeth or complete tooth loss.

Restore Your Smile With Us
Whether you have a damaged tooth that requires a crown or a missing tooth that may be suitable for a bridge, Our team can assess your smile and discuss appropriate prosthodontic solutions.

emergency care

Prompt Care for Dental Pain

Our priority during an emergency appointment is to assess the cause of your symptoms, manage pain appropriately, and determine the treatment needed to address the underlying dental problem.
If you are experiencing significant tooth pain or another dental emergency, contact Family Dental Care as soon as possible so our team can assess your condition and advise you about the next step.

Restorative Dentistry

Saving and Repairing Damaged Teeth

Restorative treatments provided are
1. Dental fillings
2. Root canal treatment.
If you have tooth pain, sensitivity, a cavity, or a damaged tooth, schedule an examination to determine whether a filling, root canal, or another restorative treatment is appropriate.

Checkups & Cleanings

Preventive Care for Your Smile

At Family Dental Care, we provide routine dental checkups and professional cleanings to help patients maintain their oral health and identify dental problems at an early stage.

we provide oral surgical services including

  • Surgical extraction

Periodontology

Comprehensive Care for Your Gums

Our periodontal services address gum-related concerns such as:

  • Gum disease
